## Description

Capital IQ is a web-based application that provides access to corporate information and financials of public and private companies, financial transactions, and professionals. In terms of coverage, Capital IQ is restructed to active companies, with series going back to about 1992. This product also includes some macroeconomic data.

## Access

Capital IQ is only available to authorized members of the Kellogg School of Management.You must use the VPN to access Capital IQ if not on the Evanston or Miami campuses. SSO is not available for this resource.

Access Capital IQ by visiting [https://www.capitaliq.com](https://www.capitaliq.com) and logging in with your individual account credentials. Note: First-time users will need to register a new account using their @kellogg email address.
