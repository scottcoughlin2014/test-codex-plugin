## Description

Datastream provides an extensive historical financial database offering global coverage of:

-   Equities
    -   Historical data for over 103,000 active equities plus over 166,000 inactive equities covering 100 developed and emerging markets with over 200 fields including prices, volumes, market capitalization, earnings, dividend and corporate action data, plus classification data based on ICB. Data is sourced directly from exchanges, leading international and local suppliers, and published reports.
    -   Coverage starting dates depend on the topic: Pricing data goes back to the 1960s, Index data to the 1950s, and Index Constituents to the 1980s.
-   Economics
    -   Datastream Economics provides comprehensive coverage of national economies, from the market moving indicators to fully detailed economic and financial accounts.
    -   Coverage goes back to 1910
    -   Similar to Global Insight Data
-   Commodities
    -   Explore more than 140,000 commodity series, with coverage from 183 sources and exchanges including Goldman Sachs, AIG and CRB.
    -   Coverage goes back to 1951
-   Futures
    -   1972-Present
    -   Datastream provides all available end-of-day pricing and trading data for both active and inactive futures. In addition, many continuous series exist for various term structures.

Datastream draws on a variety of resources, including the national statistical agencies, central banks, international organizations, as well as consulting companies and research institutes.

Frequency and coverage: Depending on the type of series, data is available on a daily, weekly, monthly, or annual basis. Historical coverage depends on the type of series and the country; some series start as early as 1950.

## Access

These data are available via [Wharton Research Data Services](https://wrds-web.wharton.upenn.edu) (WRDS). WRDS offers both a web interface, for small interactive queries, and the Linux-based WRDS Cloud for running batch programs. Datastream on WRDS is organized in four separate products: Equities, Economics, Commodities, and Futures, described in further detail above. .
