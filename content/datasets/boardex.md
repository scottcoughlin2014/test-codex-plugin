## Description

BoardEx is a database that includes profiles of business executives worldwide (including some demographics, education and career history, compensation, board and committee memberships, etc.), as well as the connections among them.

## Coverage and Matching

Generally, 1999 is the start year for BoardEx data coverage. This includes director profiles, networks, and compensation/wealth.

According to a BoardEx representative, as of early October 2013:

"We fully analyse 9,800+ US organisations split between 3,000 private and 6,800 listed, 3,000+ UK including 400 private companies, 1,500+ European, 800+ Australian, 500+ Chinese, 350+ Indian and approximately 1,000+ from the rest of the world.

Organisations through which individuals connect include charities, clubs, social and cultural organisations, government, universities and private companies. These total in excess of 950,000.

We have over 550,000 fully detailed profiles and networks, all of which are of people who are influential and connected via business, politics, the arts, charities and education."

A "fully analyzed" organization is one for which BoardEx has information regarding their compensation, committee structures, etc. Those 16,950 organizations are private and public companies. Charities, universities, clubs, etc. are not included in the fully analyzed count. The other 950,000+ organizations are called "connecting organizations" by BoardEx; coverage of their boards and leadership will be partial.

BoardEx includes company [ISIN](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Securities_Identification_Number), ticker, sector, main index, and name as of the last annual report or filing. CUSIP numbers are not included due to licensing issues with the S&P CUSIP division. However, ISIN numbers can be "converted" to CUSIP by removing the leading country code and trailing check digit; refer to the ISIN Wikipedia page.
