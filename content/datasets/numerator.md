## Description

Numerator is a market research firm that collects data from consumers’ use of purchase-tracking apps and subsequent survey panels. Kellogg’s Numerator data are longitudinal purchase histories at the individual consumer level. Kellogg has data from January 1, 2019 and will receive updates through December 31, 2023. Individual attributes include all demographics, psychographics, and media consumption plus “premium people groups:” pet, mom + baby, and health.
