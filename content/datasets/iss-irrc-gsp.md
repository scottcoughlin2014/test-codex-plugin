## Description

The database tracks shareholder resolutions related to corporate governance (though not social policy), their sponsors, the issue, company name, meeting date, votes for and against, and outcome, and covers about 5,000 companies annually. It is available from 1997 through 2005.

The licensing agreement between Kellogg and RiskMetrics (IRRC) allows access only to regular Kellogg faculty (no visitors) and Kellogg doctoral students. Do not copy and share these data with anyone. We acquired two sets of data, one in 2006, for data from 1997 to 2005, and one in mid-2012, for the data from 2006 through 2011.
</P>

<p align="left">
The database tracks shareholder resolutions related to corporate governance (though not social policy), their sponsors, the issue, company name, meeting date, votes for and against, and outcome, and covers about 5,000 companies annually. The data for 1997 through 2005 consist of one Excel spreadsheet with multiple tabs, each with one year's worth of shareholder resolutions for the companies covered, from 1997 to 2005.
</P>

<p align="left">
Due to changes in the ownership structure of IRRC (it was acquired by ISS and later renamed "Riskmetrics"), the data changed in 2011. The codes to track different shareholder proposals are different in the 2011 data.
</P>

<p align="left">
There is some shareholder proposal data from 1972 through 1996 which the School has not purchased. These data are very sparse: for 1973, for example, they have only 40 shareholder proposals. 
</P>
