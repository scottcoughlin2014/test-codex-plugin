## Description

Screenlake’s data captures both time spent on mobile apps and the appearance of specific keywords during browsing. The data is dervived from a large panel of U.S. and international users who have screen management software installed. The data will be updated monthly through the end of 2026, with a one-month lag. The keyword list includes ~2,500 (and growing) terms spanning celebrities, brands, and sports teams. Because it only tracks pre-specified keywords, it can observe behavior across many apps, including messaging platforms like SMS and WhatsApp. It also records when users view ads and the ad content
