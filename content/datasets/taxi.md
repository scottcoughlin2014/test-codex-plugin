## Description

This dataset was obtained through a Freedom of Information Law (FOIL) request from the New York City Taxi & Limousine Commission (NYCT&L). It covers 2013 taxi operations in New York City.

[Database Documentation](https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/rs/data/taxi.aspx) includes tables and field attributes.

## Access

Taxi files are available to current Kellogg faculty and doctoral students for use in academic research. This data is available on [Kellogg Data Hosting](https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/academics-research/research-support/computing/kellogg-data-hosting/).
