## Description

Kellogg's Proxycurl dataset is a one-time snapshot of 170 million public LinkedIn Profiles.

## Documentation

[Data feed descriptions](https://nuwildcat.sharepoint.com/:f:/s/KSM-RSDataDocumentation/Eo8pOuQJCEFGsqGO8ko8pUIBUB-LIrBydai5NjvHvOIp2A?e=KC9tBm) include field names, descriptions and examples for categories including personal profile information, experience, and education.

## Access

Access is restricted to members of the Northwestern University community. Please email [Kellogg Research Support](mailto:rs@kellogg.northwestern.edu) to obtain access.
