## Description

ISS Securities Class Action Services (ISS SCAS) offers a complete end-to-end litigation research and claims filing solution covering equities and fixed income securities across all markets.

## Access

These data are available on the Kellogg Linux Cluster under the **/kellogg/data/SCAS** directory.  
It contains two plain text files that contain a header row and are delimited by the pipe (|) character. An Excel data dictionary is also there.  
  
Note: Our licensing agreement runs through April, 2024. You may continue to work with the data beyond then only to modify or reproduce papers or working papers that were published during the license period.
