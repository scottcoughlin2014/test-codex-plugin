## Description

Private health insurance enrollment and premium data covering all US states, provided by the National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC), 2001-2014. Does not include self-insured/ERISA enrollees.

## Access

Current Northwestern faculty and doctoral students wishing to use the hospital financial data should contact the Kellogg Research Support team by emailing [rs@kellogg.northwestern.edu](mailto:rs@kellogg.northwestern.edu).
