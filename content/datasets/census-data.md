## Description

U.S. Census Bureau serves America as the leading source of quality data about our people, business and economy.

## Access

Access demographic, economic, geographic data, and more on the [Census website](https://www.census.gov/data/data-tools.html).

Research Support hosts some Census data locally on [Kellogg Data Hosting](https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/academics-research/research-support/computing/kellogg-data-hosting/). To inquire about this resource, please contact [Research Support](mailto:rs@kellogg.northwestern.edu).
