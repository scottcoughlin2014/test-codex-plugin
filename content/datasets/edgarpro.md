## Description

EDGAR Online offers company data and public filings for equities, mutual funds, and other publicly traded assets going back to 1994.

EDGAR Online offers the following data:

-   Fundamentals
-   SEC Filings
-   Institutional Ownership
-   Insider Trade Data
-   IPO/SPO
-   E-Prospectus
-   Money Market Data
-   Quotes and Charts
