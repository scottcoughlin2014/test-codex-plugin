## Description

DealScan database contains comprehensive historical information on loan pricing and contracts details, terms, and conditions. It contains basically loans. However, in addition to loans data, LPC DealScan also provides contract information for high yield bonds, private placements and hybrid financial structures. DealScan data is compiled from SEC filings and public documents (10Ks, 10Qs, 8Ks and registration statements), loan syndicators as well as other internal sources.  
Description courtesy of WRDS.
